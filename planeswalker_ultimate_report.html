How It Works

Doubling Season causes planeswalkers to enter with double their starting loyalty counters. This means a planeswalker can potentially activate their ultimate ability immediately if: Starting Loyalty × 2 ≥ |Ultimate Cost|

For example, Jace, the Mind Sculptor has 3 starting loyalty and a -12 ultimate. With Doubling Season: 3 × 2 = 6 loyalty, which is less than 12, so he cannot ultimate immediately.
